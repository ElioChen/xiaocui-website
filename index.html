About

Glycan Biology × Chemical Tools

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical Biology at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Ran Xie. My research develops precise chemical tools to control cell-surface glycosylation in space and time, with applications ranging from fundamental glycobiology to cancer immunotherapy.

My work has produced two first-author papers in J. Am. Chem. Soc., including a genetically encoded photoactivatable glycoenzyme platform for single-cell glycan editing, and a traceless protein-selective labeling strategy that established the "edit-and-leave" principle for accurate glycosylation biology.

In 2025, I was selected as one of 30 PhD students nationwide to attend the 74th Lindau Nobel Laureate Meeting in Germany. I hold two competitive grants as project leader, including a National Natural Science Foundation of China award.

PhD Research

Research Focus

💡

Photoactivatable Glycoenzyme Platform

Site-specific incorporation of a photocaged unnatural amino acid (ONBY) into glycoenzymes via genetic code expansion, enabling >80% activity recovery with 5 min UV irradiation. Extended to upconversion nanoparticle-assisted deep-tissue editing in vivo.

🎯

Traceless Protein-Selective Labeling

Dual-regulated editing platform (chemical inhibition + light-triggered release) using ligand–photocleavable linker–glycoenzyme conjugates. Established the "edit-and-leave" principle, revealing how prolonged enzyme residence perturbs target protein turnover and dimerization.

🫀

Cancer Immunotherapy Applications

Precision glycoengineering tools for controlled modulation of immune checkpoints (PD-L1 and others) on cancer cell surfaces, offering new avenues for targeted cancer immunotherapy.

🔭

Glycan Imaging & Visualization

Development of spatiotemporally controlled strategies for visualizing and manipulating dynamic cell-surface glycans in living cells and animal models, enabling new insights into glycan function.
